Patient Journey
From WhatsApp message to follow-up — fully automated
1

Patient Messages on WhatsApp

"Hi, mujhe bukhar hai" — Bot detects Hindi, asks for details, finds the right doctor, shows available slots with wait times. Appointment confirmed in under 2 minutes.

2

Arrives at Hospital → Kiosk Check-in

Enters phone number or ABHA at the kiosk tablet. System recognizes them, shows token and directions. No receptionist interaction needed.

3

Waits with Real-time Updates

TV screen outside doctor's room shows queue. Voice announces next patient in 5 languages. WhatsApp sends position updates.

4

Doctor Consultation — AI-Assisted

Doctor sees AI-generated briefing: complaint, history, allergies, medications. One-tap to prescribe, order tests, refer. Complete in half the time.

5

Auto Bill → Insurance → Follow-up

Bill generated automatically. Insurance claim submitted. Medication reminders sent. Follow-up scheduled. Feedback collected after 2 hours.
